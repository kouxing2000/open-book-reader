# Privacy Policy — Open Book Reader

The policy is published at **<https://openbook.peach-studio.com/privacy.html>**, rendered from
[`site/privacy.html`](site/privacy.html) in this repo. That page is the single source, and the
URL the Chrome Web Store listing points at.

This file is deliberately a pointer rather than a copy: nothing here generates one document from
the other, so a second copy drifts out of date while still reading as authoritative.
