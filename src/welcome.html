You're all set

Open Book Reader turns any article into a calm, two-page reading view.

  1. Pin it so it's one click away

    New extensions hide in Chrome's toolbar menu. Pin Open Book Reader so you can always reach it.

    Click 🧩 in the toolbar, then 📌 pin Open Book Reader

  2. Open any article

    Go to a news story, blog post, or long page you'd like to read without the clutter.

  3. Read it like a book

    Click the 📖 toolbar icon, or press Alt+B for text (Alt+Shift+B for images). Use the arrow keys to turn pages, and Esc to close.

Try it on a sample article → Opens a clean article in a new tab — activate the reader there to see it in action.

🔒 Everything happens on your device. Open Book Reader collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer.
